# Test: diagnose mode, black spots in a bedroom corner (2026-11-21)

Symptom: "There are black spots in the corner of the bedroom, up by the ceiling." Old Copenhagen flat. This tests step 0.

**Questions asked (one at a time):** which wall, inside or outside? Since when, and worse in winter? Is a wardrobe or bed against it? How do you air out and dry laundry? Any changes lately, like new windows? (Photo and building age came with the first message.)
Answers used: outer wall, north side, top corner, started in November, a wardrobe stands 3 cm from the wall, laundry dries indoors, windows are new (2024). The spots are about 20 × 30 cm.

**Likely causes:**
1. **Condensation on a cold corner (most likely).** Fits: outer north wall, top corner (coldest spot), started when it got cold, laundry indoors, tight new windows, wardrobe blocks warm air.
   Test: hygrometer for a week. Over ~60 % relative humidity most evenings points here. Foil test: water on the room side.
2. **Damp coming through the wall** (bad gutter, cracked render). Test: foil test shows water behind the foil; worse after rain, not after showers. Ask the housing association to check the gutter and facade.
3. **Leak from above.** Unlikely: no stain spreading from the ceiling, and no change after the neighbour showers. If the ceiling shows a ring or the spot grows fast: landlord or association, and insurance.

**Not DIY if:** the test points at 2 or 3, or the mould comes back after cleaning, or it grows beyond ~1 m².

**Handover to the plan (if cause 1):** "Clean the mould and stop the condensation." The plan then gives: clean with a mould remover (gloves, mask, window open), move the wardrobe 5-10 cm out, dry laundry outside or by an open window, air out 3 × 10 minutes a day, and keep the bedroom at least 18 °C.
Sourcing then prices only the hygrometer, the mould remover and a P2 mask.

**Result:** one question at a time got enough to rank the causes. Each cause has a test under 100 kr, and the plan only starts after the test. The pro and housing-association line is clear.
